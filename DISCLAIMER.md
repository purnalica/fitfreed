## Current development boundary

FitFreed has no released or supported version yet. The current application is intended only for independently constructed synthetic data. Do not use it with a real personal export until the project publishes a supported public release and activates the version-matched compatible-data, backup, recovery, installation, and update guidance.

Development builds, snapshots, and unsigned packages may be incomplete, unstable, incompatible with later versions, or removed without a migration path. They are not suitable as the only copy of any information.

## Data integrity and availability

Fitness-platform exports and FitFreed libraries may contain sensitive and irreplaceable history. Software defects, incompatible provider-format changes, interrupted imports, storage failures, migrations, or updates may cause information to be omitted, duplicated, misinterpreted, rendered inaccurate, or lost.

Before using a released FitFreed version with personal data:

- retain an untouched copy of every original provider export;
- maintain independent, tested backups of the FitFreed library;
- verify important results against their source before relying on them; and
- follow the compatibility, installation, update, migration, backup, and recovery instructions for that exact version.

FitFreed is not a backup service, archival guarantee, or substitute for preserving original source data.
