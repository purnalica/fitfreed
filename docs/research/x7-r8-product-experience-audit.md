## Audit question

The audit tries to falsify whether a person can complete this sequence without knowing FitFreed's architecture or
provider representations:

> understand the local ownership proposition → obtain or choose the right export → import with confidence → recognize
> the resulting history → find a sport, date, or session → understand one workout → investigate its route, signals,
> structure, and ranges when supported → answer a comparative question → keep, rerun, duplicate, and export the useful
> result → change application preferences without changing evidence → recover from ordinary errors and interruption

Every surface is challenged first with the question a person brought to it. Controls, secondary evidence, exact
tables, provenance, and diagnostics pass only when they follow a concise answer and remain reachable on request.

## Method

1. Trace every ordinary action to its production component, application command, use case, port, and persistence or
   output boundary. A visible field with no verified lower-layer outcome is a defect.
2. Replay the complete release-shaped functional campaign with production application paths and synthetic data. Check
   every input, validation, save, cancel, add, remove, move, selector, return origin, reload, reimport, migration,
   backup, restore, export, update, and recovery outcome rather than control presence.
3. Inspect the actual packaged WebView in `en-US` and `es-ES`, light and dark, wide, short-laptop and compact windows,
   100% through 200% content zoom, reduced motion, keyboard operation, accessible semantics, long localized values,
   dense histories, and every empty, loading, partial, stale, invalid, cancelled, failed, and completed state.
4. Evaluate first-use comprehension and expert depth separately. The first useful result must be recognizable within
   minutes; exact values, authorship, provenance, local control, and data exit must remain available deliberately.
5. Classify only observable conformance gaps against accepted requirements. Correct every accepted critical or major
   finding at its causal boundary, preserve behavior assertions, and repeat the affected journey plus the whole audit.

## Severity

- **Critical:** the ownership journey, data safety, or a required exit path fails; no candidate may be handed off.
- **Major:** a central capability exists but normal use materially impairs comprehension, trust, navigation, or control.
- **Minor:** the task completes clearly and safely, but a bounded refinement would improve efficiency or finish.
