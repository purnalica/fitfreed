# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

FitFreed is committed to a respectful, inclusive, accessible, and harassment-free community. Participation is welcome regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, nationality, appearance, religion, or personal circumstances.

This commitment applies to repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, reviews, discussions, events, private project communication, and public representation of the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction professionally.
- Respect different experience levels, communication styles, languages, and accessibility needs.
- Protect personal fitness data, credentials, private communications, and vulnerability details.
- Credit contributions accurately and avoid pressuring anyone to disclose identity or private information.
- Stop behavior when asked and help restore a safe, productive discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, demeaning language, deliberate misgendering, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information.
- Submitting personal exports, health information, routes, credentials, or confidential reports without explicit authorization.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or assists an investigation.
- Using project status, maintainer access, or technical authority to bypass these standards.

## Reporting

Do not report a conduct incident in a public issue or discussion.

During the private bootstrap stage, contact the project owner through an existing private collaboration channel. If the project owner is involved in the concern, use a private channel to another `purnalica` organization owner. A dedicated confidential conduct-reporting route, with at least two eligible recipients, must be named here before public community participation begins.

Reports should include only the information needed to understand the incident, the affected project space, available evidence, immediate safety concerns, and any requested protective measure. Reporter identity and report details will be shared only with the people required to investigate and respond, subject to legal or safety obligations.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. Responses will be proportionate to the behavior, impact, context, prior incidents, and safety needs, and may include:

1. A private clarification or correction request.
2. A formal warning and required behavior change.
3. Temporary restriction from project spaces or responsibilities.
4. Permanent removal from the community or maintainer role.
5. Escalation to a hosting provider or relevant authority when required for safety or law.

No project role exempts a participant from enforcement. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed, and a person implicated in a report cannot decide its outcome.

## Appeals and records

A person subject to a formal restriction may request review by an uninvolved project or organization owner. Maintainers will retain the minimum confidential record needed for consistent enforcement and will not publish case details merely to justify an action.
