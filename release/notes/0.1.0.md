## Highlights

- Import supported Polar Flow takeout families into a provider-neutral local library.
- Explore daily activity, training summaries and structure, private local routes with exact recorded points, supported temporal signals with exact samples, primary sleep, and dated nightly recovery independently or through one aligned longitudinal view.
- Reimport exact or later exports without duplicating canonical history, while retaining explicit conflicts and complete artifact-family coverage.
- Use localized English (United States) and Spanish (Spain) interfaces with exact accessible alternatives for every visualization.

## Compatibility

FitFreed 0.1.0 supports Apple Silicon on macOS 15.0 or later. Package metadata, executable deployment target, release contracts, and the hosted Apple Silicon campaign share that boundary. Performance acceptance uses the passing hosted macOS and clean local Apple Silicon campaigns and does not claim identical timing across every supported Mac. The included compatibility matrix declares direct library migration from SQLite schemas 1 through 17 and no prior application-version upgrade baseline because FitFreed has not released an earlier application.

Input compatibility is limited to the documented Polar Flow daily-activity, training summary, exercise/lap/pause structure, route, and supported interval-signal series, split sleep-result and sleep-score, and dated nightly-recovery shapes. Recognition of another artifact family does not mean that its records are imported.

## Privacy and data

Fitness history, source provenance, locale preference, and update policy state remain in the local FitFreed library by default. FitFreed has no account, analytics, or synchronization service. Update checks do not transmit imported facts, provider data, locale, library schema, installation identity, or usage data.

## Known limitations

- Garmin and other providers are not implemented.
- External route maps, provider-defined zones, unsupported training-signal types, and undated recovery samples are not included.
- There is no supported user-controlled backup, restore, portable normalized export, or in-application library deletion workflow.
- Compatibility is limited to the documented provider-export shapes; recognition of another family does not mean its records are imported.

## Installation and recovery

Verify the complete version-matched evidence before mounting the DMG. Follow only the installation procedure supplied by the same distribution channel; never disable Gatekeeper globally or alter system-wide security settings. Preserve each original provider ZIP independently.

Moving `FitFreed.app` to the Trash leaves the separate local library intact. Automatic application-and-library preservation immediately before an authorized update is update-recovery state, not a general backup facility.
